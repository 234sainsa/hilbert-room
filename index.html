HILBERT ROOM

A small state-vector quantum circuit simulator. Build a circuit, run shots, and look at the counts and the exact state probabilities.

Simulation only. Every result on this page is computed on a classical computer. Measurement outcomes are drawn from a classical random number generator (the browser's crypto.getRandomValues, or a seeded pseudo-random generator). They are not quantum randomness and do not come from quantum hardware.
